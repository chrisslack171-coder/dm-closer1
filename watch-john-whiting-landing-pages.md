## 3. Frameworks, principles, and rules

### Page psychology & copy
- **The Peripheral-Vision Test**: look off to the side of the screen - can you still read the headline? Main points must read "like McDonald's golden arches." His headline is ~10 words.
- **Lead with the Promise of Outcome**: headline = outcome the reader wants and doesn't have. Sophisticated markets are outcome-driven; mass markets are pain-driven.
- **New Opportunity Positioning**: "make your [stuff] sound like a new opportunity." Use "new," "free," "install," and the year. Caveat: novelty alone stops working in saturated, sophisticated markets.
- **Basic Headline Formula**: "get benefit in time period with new mechanism without obstacle." Client example: "This simple ad system will get you one to three clients in the next seven days or you don't pay."
- **Believable Time/Number Ranges**: "$1M in 5 minutes" too fast, "$1M in 10 years" too slow. Ranges (15-30 hours, 30-50% more growth) read as credible.
- **"Could" vs "Will"** for skeptical audiences.
- **Speak Above Your Avatar**: "You will always attract below the level that you speak to. If I want to attract people who make 10K a month, I got to speak to at least 25."
- **Never promise the result**: "I never say I will get you to 50-100K a month. I say my goal is this."
- **Articulate the problem better than they can** - they automatically assume you have the solution.
- **Copy Hooks**: lines that sell the next unit of consumption ("Take 90 seconds to read this letter. You'll be glad you did." / "P.S. Read the rest of this page…")
- **Design for Skimmers**: the bold/big text alone must tell the whole story "as a skimmer on the toilet."
- **Open Loops / Closed Loops**: constantly open new loops (an unlabeled dashboard screenshot opens "what software is that?").
- **The Big Domino + little dominoes**: one master belief, then ~100 retargeting content pieces knock the smaller beliefs from different angles.
- **Matchers vs Mismatchers / Reverse Psychology**: "If I ask you to accept something, you'll reject it. If I ask you to reject something, you'll accept it." Hence anti-hype delivery and "impossible… that's what all my clients thought until…" headlines.
- **Sell them what they want, then tell the truth inside**: "The front end is the bait that they want. Once they're in, tell them the truth and provide the actual real solution."
- **Front-end pain vs secondary benefit**: market to the pain on their mind now (time), sub-stitch the one they think they can solve (money).
- **Call-out format**: "Why your million-dollar hardscaping company still runs like your first $100,000 year - and the blueprint that solves it."
- **ICP Narrative Crafting**: write your ideal customer's whole life timeline. "If you can't explain that timeline for your ICP, it's hard to market to them."
- **Never use the word "easy"** - "Easy attracts shitty people. Use simple."
- **Name the CTA after the desire**: "Book my get-out-of-the-weeds call," never "Book your 20-minute builder blueprint call."
- **Less is more / one ask**: "The more information you provide at any step, the more opportunities you give them to object."

### Visual hierarchy & design
- **Show, Don't Tell** and **VAK**: give every offer in visual, auditory, kinesthetic formats; mirror modality words.
- **Color Coding**: red = pain, green = goal, purple-blue gradient = AI; old way dark/red, new way light/white/green. He circled his dashboard number in white because red is reserved for pain on that page.
- **Old Way / New Way Graphic** (credited to Alan Sultanic): old way named badly ("Brute Force Selling"), wordy, gritty font, ~10 red steps; new way half the steps, clean font, whitespace. Works even unread.
- **Gen 1 / Gen 2 / Gen 3 identity graphic**: Manual → AI-assisted ("the illusion of progress") → AI agent model. Two bad buckets avoid pigeonholing. Total skim text: eight words.
- **Rule of Three identities**: "Two's not enough. Four is too many." Ghost / Wantrepreneur / Bulletproof Entrepreneur - "Which of those three do you want to be?"
- **Above the Fold + Induce the Scroll**: CTA fully above the fold on mobile; his head and arrows peeking up induce scrolling. Mobile-first always.
- **Circles and arrows direct the eye**; **movement creates engagement** (auto-play video, scrolling strips).
- **Testimonial rules**: order by fame; names smallest; play button = proof it's real; one curated quote per card in ICP language; identical card sizes; 2-wide mobile stacking; all under ~1 minute (Descript); each must work as the only one watched; higher-end testimonials only ("If you display zero-to-hero ones, you will attract zeros"); don't over-prove.
- **Photos**: always you WITH the famous person; expressions model the intended emotion; family/baby/dog over cars ("babies convert"); understated flex ("letting you know without letting you know").
- **Title case headlines, normal-case captions, no all caps** ("All caps is yelling").
- **Illusion of Choice**: every option they pick is a win for you.
- **Anti-AI-slop aesthetic**: template-identical AI-built pages signal "you mailed it in"; hand-crafted pages signal craft.

### Funnel mechanics
- **Top of funnel**: get the right people in; optimize for the highest-intent objective.
- **Middle of funnel**: "get them to consume seven hours of content, not convert. Not convert, not convert." No conversion buttons on true MOF retargeting. Retarget only pre-qualified audiences (his: ~3,500 people; "your retargeting audience is 167,000 people… you're an idiot").
- **One CTA per page/ad**: "Funnels don't have holes in the side. They got one in the bottom."
- **Funnel Complexity Ladder**: follower/DM funnel = 1, DM ads = 2, book-a-call = 2-3, "SLO is a 10. Not even a nine. If you can't dial in a two, you're above your head."
- **When to add an SLO**: only when everything else is dialed - "If you don't know, you can't afford it."
- **Webinar = VSL + Offer Doc** split across mediums; same sequence as a sales call.
- **Ad-page congruence**; **result is senior to theory** ("If it works, forget what I say").
- **Split-test method**: element tests in GHL/ClickFunnels A/B; whole-new-angle tests get a separate campaign; kill what doesn't beat control (his failed "2x your ROAS" angle).
- **Broken Windows principle** (via Dan Kennedy): one broken link makes them ask "What else is wrong?" Audit everything a prospect can find.
- **Get ahead of AI research**: prospects will ChatGPT you; pre-frame hallucinations, provide the verified source.
- **Legitimacy signals**: phone number at top of low-ticket pages (his: Google Voice → support email voicemail).
- **Sniper rifle vs shotgun / Jenga**: few moving parts, dial one meticulously; pull out one brick (sales calls) at a time. Launch threshold: "I don't see how this couldn't do pretty well is really my good-enough point."

## 6. Page anatomies walked through

### A. VSL landing page (Bulletproof Boardroom)
1. Headline readable in one fixation: outcome + "new AI agent coaching model" + "no calls, no team, no stress" + year; ~10 words
2. Autoplaying VSL, engineered first frame: circling $1.4M net profit on a dashboard; awards, office, watch in view; then family/dog shots
3. CTA: "Ready to enjoy your business again?" → DM the word "bulletproof"
4. Pre-frame below CTA: "I don't have setters or closers in the DMs" (ManyChat automation, disclosed)
5. Testimonial wall: 20 of 850 videos, ranked by fame, identical cards, one curated quote each, 2-wide mobile stacking
6. Disclaimers placed high ("the more you say nothing's guaranteed, the more trustworthy you are")
7. Single forward option - "all roads lead to here"

### B. Propaganda Machine SLO sales page (fullest anatomy)
1. Headline (split-test winner, narrative): "The AI content and ad system closed $294K last month" + red sub-headline "without sales calls or long DM convos" + "in less than two hours." Vanilla control for most people: "Install the content ad system that pre-sells clients so they show up ready to buy."
2. Above the fold on mobile: CTA fully visible; head + arrows induce scroll; phone number for legitimacy
3. "From the desk of John Whiting" letter + copy hook ("Take 90 seconds…")
4. Proof block first: dashboard screenshot, number circled in white, earnings disclaimer; doubles as ascension-priming
5. Anti-positioning: "I'm not an AI marketing guru"
6. Client results in a 2x2 grid with highlighted phrases
7. Story headlines readable by skimming alone: "In 2023 I grew to hate my seven-figure coaching business" → photos with famous clients → "Now I get pre-sold clients showing up ready to buy" → "An AI agent runs 95% of the system" → signature → "P.S. Read the rest of this page…"
8. "Introducing the Propaganda Machine": clean product mockup; four scrolling content strips subliminally reading "join, buy, obey, pay in full" (the They Live homage)
9. "The Propaganda Effect" skim-line benefits
10. Old Way / New Way graphic: "Brute Force Selling," graffiti font, ~10 red steps vs ~4 clean black bubbles
11. "Imagine this" future-pacing: the dream DM (opens the "what link?" loop)
12. Three simple steps + "Stop chasing prospects. Use AI to make them buy."
13. Purple/AI section: "YOUR pre-trained AI agent" ("Notice I didn't say A pre-trained agent - I've already given it to you in your mind"); AI badge + 30-day guarantee under every buy button
14. Six bonuses, each named as a thing you need (Offer Optimizer, 98 Buying Beliefs, AI Agent Trust Audit, Conversion Codex, his book, The AI Agent Business Model)
15. Gen 1/2/3 identity graphic (pre-frames the backend offer)
16. "Become a master of modern persuasion in less than two hours" recap + everything-you-get summary
17. FAQ + deliberately limited bottom testimonials ("too many creates more doubt")

### C. Book-a-call page prescription (given live to a client whose page did ~$1.5M)
Headline (pain-named: "Sick of everything running through you?") → VSL → embedded white calendar matching the page (no popup, no button) → 3-6 identically formatted testimonials under the calendar. Calendar event named after the desire ("get-out-of-the-weeds call"). Everything else deleted.

### D. Opt-in / lead-magnet template
Google-doc-first mockup, H1/H2/H3 + bullets; headline = "Do you want [big goal] without [obstacle]?"; button = "Yes, I want [big goal]"; segmenting qualifier line for the specific avatar.
