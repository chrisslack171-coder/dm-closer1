## Posting notes stolen from his system

1. **Comment-gate every tutorial.** One keyword per video, all caps in the caption. His comments regularly beat his likes — that ratio is the growth engine.
2. **Repost your winners.** He re-ran his time-freeze reel 48 hours later and took 67k more views. When a video outperforms your median, repost it within a week with a new first frame.
3. **Alternate engines.** Tutorial (engagement) → spectacle (reach) → tutorial. Never two spectacles in a row.
4. **One sentence per beat.** Every winning transcript above is under 100 words. Write the script, then cut every clause that doesn't advance the recipe.
