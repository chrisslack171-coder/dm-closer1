The 5 patterns behind every winner

  • Result before explanation. Every tutorial opens with ~5 seconds of the jaw-dropping finished output, then the signature line "Let's make it together." The hook is the proof, not a promise.
  • Comment-gated lead magnet on every winner. "Comment MEGA PROMPT / COFFEE / PRODUCT" — comment counts often beat like counts (5,559 comments vs 4,416 likes on the 33x winner). Comments are the reach engine.
  • One short sentence per step. The tutorials compress a multi-tool workflow into 6–8 spoken beats, zero filler. Watch time stays high because every second advances the recipe.
  • Two engines, deliberately split. Talky tutorials + comment gate = engagement plays; wordless spectacle showcases (dental reels, 13.8x and 5.2x) = raw reach plays that sell services via DMs. He alternates them.
  • He reposts winners. The Aug 29 time-freeze reel is a near-verbatim repost of the Aug 27 one — winning content gets re-run, not retired.
