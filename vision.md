## Mission
Teach people who are trying to sell digital products online how to close conversations **honestly** — serve, don't chase — so they can make real income without turning into the pushy, sleazy salesperson everyone can smell coming.

## The real reason
Freedom. Getting off the grind — out of the quota-and-commission machine where someone else owns your time and caps your income. Building something that's *yours*, where the work compounds and the hours are your own instead of being spent on another company's sales floor.

## Why this exists
Burned out on field sales. Years of grinding doors and phones for someone else's quota — closing strangers who didn't ask for you, at the hardest door there is — and realizing the skill that made all those closes possible was the one asset worth keeping. The pivot: take that field-proven closing system, translate it to selling online, and build a business on it instead of renting the skill out by the hour.

## Consequences if nothing changes
Stay on the floor — trading time for commission, income capped, skill stuck at one conversation at a time, no equity in anything of your own. The closing ability stays a tool someone else profits from, and the freedom stays a "someday." Coast and the door just keeps revolving.

A real online business that runs on products, not your calendar: free tools pulling people in, a low-ticket course converting them, and a community keeping them — with an audience, recurring income, and time that belongs to you. **Built to scale, not to trade hours** — the skill reaches thousands at once instead of one door (or one coaching call) at a time. _(Set the specific targets — MRR, audience, buyers — as they firm up.)_

## Backstory — how you got here

### Past situation
Grinding field / door-to-door sales — cold, face-to-face, with people who didn't book and didn't want you there. Over 1,000 closes earned at the hardest door in the game, all of it for someone else's quota. _(Add the specific role / timeline if you want it concrete.)_

### Past pains
Time owned by the sales floor. Income capped by someone else's pay structure. No equity in anything — every close made the company money and left you with a commission and another door to knock. Burned out, and aware the grind had no ceiling worth climbing toward. _(Make this as raw and specific as you're comfortable with — it's the part people connect to.)_

### Past desires
Freedom and autonomy. Time that's yours. A business of your own where the closing skill builds *your* thing instead of renting it out by the hour — and the upside isn't capped by a commission table.

### Epiphany
The close is a transferable skill. What works at the hardest cold door — the read, the push-pull, the takeaway, the silence after the price — is the same spine that works in a DM and on a call. If you can close strangers who didn't ask for you, you can teach people to close the warm leads already in their inbox — and do it honestly. Same system, different room, and online it scales past one conversation at a time.
