## Headline offer
A low-ticket course that teaches people selling digital products online how to **close the conversation without being pushy** — built on a closing system earned over 1,000+ field closes, not borrowed from a guru.

## Tiers

### The Tools (DM Closer + hub) — free / low-cost entry
- **For**: Anyone trying to sell online who freezes or fumbles mid-conversation — the widest door.
- **Includes**: The DM Closer and the other tools on the hub / Stan store. Paste a door, a DM, or a call objection and get the read plus your next moves, all the way to the close.
- **Outcome**: They stop guessing in real time — they know the next move in a live DM or call, and they feel the system work before they ever pay for it.

### The Course — _$47–$97 one-time (working price)_
- **For**: People who've felt the tools work and are ready to actually *learn* to close — own the system, not just lean on the tool.
- **Includes**: The full closing system taught end to end — The Read (what kind of lead this is + qualifying), the fork (book / down-sell / nurture / triage), handling the objection chain, the price drop and the silence, and the door-to-DM-to-call through-line that ties it together.
- **Outcome**: A repeatable system they can run on any conversation, every time — closing as the calm, in-control human in the room instead of the desperate one.

> _Pricing is a working number, not locked. Adjust here once it's set; the scriptwriter reads from this file._

## What's NOT in the offer

_Things you explicitly don't promise. Keeps scripts honest._

- **No income claims or guarantees.** No "make $10k/mo," no promised numbers. This is a skill you build.
- **No leads or traffic.** This isn't lead-gen — it's about converting the conversations and attention you already have.
- **No done-for-you closing.** Nobody sells for you. You learn to do it yourself.
- **No 1:1 / done-with-you coaching.** This is deliberate, not a gap. The whole point is to scale the skill past one conversation at a time — you get the system through the tools and the course, not hours of my time.
- **No magic-bullet / overnight anything.** It's reps and a system, not a hack.
