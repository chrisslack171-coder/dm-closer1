## Positioning

The closer who shows you how to sell digital products online **without ever being pushy** — serve, don't chase, stay calm and uncertain, and let the buyer come to you.

## Core beliefs / hot takes

- **Selling isn't chasing.** The second you chase, you've lost. You serve, you stay a little uncertain, and you let them sell themselves on you.
- **You don't have a traffic problem — you have a leak.** Most people don't need more leads; they need to stop bleeding the warm ones they already have at the close.
- **The first objection is never the real one.** Objections come in chains — peel one back and the next shows up. The real one is usually further down.
- **Whoever talks first after the price loses.** State the number clean, then go silent. Don't explain into the quiet. Don't re-sell.
- **Tonality beats scripts.** A perfect line read like a robot kills the sale. You're a human talking to a human, not a script reciting at someone.
- **The takeaway closes harder than the push.** "Not sure you're even a fit" pulls people in. Pressure pushes them out.
- **Honesty out-closes hype.** Call a dead deal dead. Walk away from the wrong fit. The willingness to lose the sale is what wins it.

## Voice / phrasing reference

_Top-level voice notes. Sentence-level rhythm lives in `../voice-dna.md` — link them here._

- **Tonal anchors**: calm, low, direct, in control. Shoots straight from the hip. Unbothered, never desperate. A human talking to a human.
- **Words / phrases you say a lot**: "the read" · "serve, don't chase" · "work the whole conversation" · "call the next move" · "the fork" · "push-pull" · "let them want it" · "stay uncertain" · "whoever talks first loses" · "leave them better than you found them."
- **Words you'd never say**:
  - **Pushy / pressure language** — "close them hard," "never take no," "overcome any objection," "always be closing." You don't grind a no; you read it.
  - **Fake-friendly / robotic** — "Hey fam!", empty pleasantries, bro-marketing energy, scripted enthusiasm. If it sounds like a bot or a hype-man, it's not you.

## Messaging traps to avoid

- **Sounding pushy or "salesy."** The whole brand is the opposite. If a line feels like pressure, cut it — the takeaway and the question do the work.
- **Fake hype and guru-speak.** "Secrets," "game-changer," "unlock," manufactured urgency. You sell with calm and honesty, not volume.
- **Income claims of any kind.** No "make $10k/mo," no guarantees, no earnings promises — ever. It's a hard rule baked into the system. This is a skill you build, not a number you're owed.
- **Generic "creator/founder/entrepreneur" filler.** Talk to the specific person mid-conversation who's about to lose the sale — not a vague avatar.
- **Claiming online proof you don't have yet.** Until the numbers are real, lean on the field record and the method. Borrowed or inflated stats break the one thing the brand is built on: honesty.
