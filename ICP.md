# ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

Who you serve. The `scriptwriter` skill loads this to make sure every script is aimed at a real person — not a vague "creator" or "founder."

---

## Who you serve

People on the road to selling digital products online — creators, side-hustlers, and aspiring coaches/consultants who can get attention and conversations but keep losing the sale the moment it's time to actually close. The door is open to anyone who has (or is building) something to sell online and freezes, fumbles, or starts chasing when a real buyer shows up.

### Tighter tier (if you have one)

Coaches and consultants with a real 1:1 offer who are already getting on calls and trading in the DMs, but leaking deals at the close. They don't need permission to sell — they need the deeper read: the objection chains, the push-pull, the price-drop silence. _(Forward-looking — this becomes the premium cut as the proof and the high-touch offer come online.)_

---

## Current situation

Where they're at right now — what their life actually looks like.

**Their work / content / business:**
- Posting content and slowly building an audience, or just getting started — there's interest, but it isn't turning into money.
- Have a digital product (or one half-built in their head) and no real process to sell it. Every conversation is improvised.
- People watch, like, and DM "this is so helpful" — then go quiet. Warm leads cool off and disappear.

**Their monetization:**
- Little to no revenue, or the odd sale that they can't repeat or explain.
- Money's stuck at the conversation, not the traffic — they're getting leads and leaking every one of them at the close.
- Running on hope-marketing: post, pray, and hope someone buys without ever being asked.

**Their mindset:**
- Believe the online-income dream is real and want in — but they're allergic to feeling fake, pushy, or "salesy."
- Conflate selling with scamming, so they under-sell, soften the ask, and let buyers walk to avoid being "that guy."
- Have watched a hundred hours of content and still don't know what to actually *say* when a live person is on the other end.
- Skeptical of guru tactics and high-pressure scripts — they want something that feels like them.

**Where they consume / live online:**
- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube — short-form first. They follow creators, coaches, and "make money online" accounts.
- Hang around Stan stores, Skool/Discord communities, and free challenges. They click the link in bio.
- Drawn to hooks-and-hustle content but quietly tired of motivation with no mechanics.

**The recurring tension:**
- Someone's clearly interested in the DMs and the conversation just... dies — and they don't know what they did wrong.
- On a call they hit "I can't afford it right now" or "let me think about it" and instantly cave.
- The "I'm obviously not bad at this content thing — so why can't I get anyone to actually buy?" moment. That's when they go looking.

---

## Problems

What they're struggling with.

- Get DMs and genuine interest but have no idea what to say to move it toward a sale.
- Freeze up or go stiff and robotic the second it's time to talk money or ask for the close.
- Feel pushy and hate it — so they pull back, under-sell, and let warm leads walk away.
- No repeatable process: every door, DM, and call is winged from scratch, so results are random.
- Cave to objections — "too expensive," "let me think," "I'll get back to you" — instead of reading and handling them.
- Keep building offers and content but can't convert the attention into actual income.
- Drowning in advice yet paralyzed in the moment — they know theory, not the next move.

---

## Desires

What they want.

- Close conversations without ever feeling sleazy, fake, or pushy — to serve, not chase.
- One simple, repeatable system they can run on any door, DM, or call, every single time.
- The confidence to sit in "I can't afford it" or "let me think" and handle it instead of folding.
- Turn the audience and attention they're already building into real, consistent sales.
- Know the exact next move at every step — what to say, why it works, and when to stay quiet.
- Make their first (then repeatable) sales of a digital product online and finally feel like a real seller.
- Sell as the calm, in-control human in the room — not the desperate one.

---

## Who you don't serve

- **The pushy-tactics crowd.** Anyone hunting for manipulative, high-pressure, "never take no" scripts. This system serves, it doesn't chase — wrong house, wrong door.
- **Get-rich-quick and income-claim seekers.** People who want a guaranteed-money promise or an overnight number. There are no income claims here, ever — this is a skill you build, not a lottery ticket.
- **The eternal student who won't get in a conversation.** Tire-kickers collecting frameworks who never actually message a lead, get on a call, or do the reps. Nothing to close if you never start.
