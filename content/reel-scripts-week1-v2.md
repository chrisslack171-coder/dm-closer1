# DM Closer — Week 1 Reel Scripts (v2: Course Creators)

Repositioned for **course & digital-product creators**, closing as the wedge.
Same machine as Caroline's: broad/curiosity hook for reach → niche offer in the
caption → **comment `CLOSER` → DM** funnel. Voice: calm, direct, serve-don't-sell.
**No income or earnings claims.** Spread across the 5 content pillars.

## Day 1 — The Close (authority) · Pillar: The Close

- **Hook (say first):** "Everyone teaches you to launch. Nobody teaches you to close. That's why your launch flopped."
- **On-screen text:** Build → Launch → 🤝 Close
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "You did the work — built the course, ran the launch, got the DMs. Then someone says 'how much?' and you fumble it. The launch doesn't pay you. The *close* does. I came up at the hardest door in sales, and the same moves close a course in the DMs. Start here: never answer 'how much' with just a number."
- **Caption:** You can build it and launch it — can you close it? Comment **CLOSER** and I'll send you my DM closing system. 🤝 Save this · Follow.
- **Why:** contrarian hook names the exact gap your audience feels; your origin = instant authority.

- **Caption:** Speed in the DMs = more closes during a launch. Comment **CLOSER** for the actual words I use to close. Save this 🔖
- **Why:** broadly useful hack = max reach (her top reels were phone hacks); still on-niche; funnels.

## Day 3 — The objection line · Pillar: The Close

- **Hook:** "'Let me think about it' on your launch is never about thinking. Here's the line that flips it."
- **On-screen text:** The think-about-it close
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "When a launch lead says 'let me think about it,' don't push — lower your tone. *'Totally fair. Usually when someone says that, there's one specific thing they're unsure about. What is it for you?'* Now you're handling the real objection, not the polite no."
- **Caption:** The objection they say is rarely the real one. Comment **CLOSER** for my objection playbook. Save for your next launch.
- **Why:** one sharp, usable mechanic; curiosity + contrarian.

## Day 4 — Listicle · Pillar: Launch

- **Hook:** "3 reasons your course launch didn't sell — and it's not the price."
- **On-screen text:** 1 · 2 · 3 👇
- **Format:** listicle talking head
- **Script:** "One — your offer wasn't clear; confusion never buys. Two — no real reason to act now; 'someday' never converts. Three — you marketed hard but never *closed* the DMs. You got the 'interested' and let it sit. Reach gets attention. The close gets paid."
- **Caption:** Most failed launches die in the DMs, not the price. Comment **CLOSER** for the close-it-out scripts. 🔖 Save.
- **Why:** numbered/listicle = save-worthy (her "4 strategies / 6 hacks" format).

## Day 5 — Push-pull · Pillar: The Close

- **Hook:** "Stop chasing launch leads in the DMs. Watch what happens when you pull back."
- **On-screen text:** Push → pull
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "Give value, then take a little away. *'Honestly, not sure my program's even a fit for you yet — can I ask what you're working on?'* That bit of uncertainty makes them sell YOU on why it fits. Chasing repels. Calm closes."
- **Caption:** Desperation repels, calm closes. Comment **CLOSER** for the push-pull DMs I use. Follow for more.
- **Why:** pattern-interrupt hook; teaches your signature mechanic.

## Day 6 — The assume-close · Pillar: The Close

- **Hook:** "Never end a launch DM with 'let me know.' Do this instead."
- **On-screen text:** Two yeses, never a yes/no
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "'Let me know' hands the decision back and the deal goes cold. Once they've leaned in, assume it: *'I've got two spots opening this week — want me to get you set up Tuesday or Thursday?'* Two yeses, never a yes-or-no. You're not selling, you're scheduling."
- **Caption:** 'Let me know' is where launches die. Comment **CLOSER** for the exact close-out messages. Save this 🔖
- **Why:** bold contrarian command hook; instantly usable line.

## Day 7 — Origin story (broad relatability) · Pillar: Story/Authority

- **Hook:** "I launched a digital product with 600 followers. Here's the part nobody posts — the selling."
- **On-screen text:** The part nobody shows
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "Everyone shows the launch graphics and the 'doors are open' posts. Nobody shows the DMs — the 'how much,' the 'let me think,' the ghosting. That's where it's won or lost. I close those the same way I closed strangers at the door: agree, redirect, stay calm, never chase."
- **Caption:** The launch is the easy part. The DMs are where it's won. Comment **CLOSER** for how I close them. Save · Follow.
- **Why:** small-account relatability + curiosity = shares; ties origin to the niche.

- **~20–40 seconds.** One idea per reel.
- **Every caption ends with `Comment CLOSER`** + "Save" + "Follow."
- **Alternate reach + authority:** broad hack/story (Days 2, 7) with teaching (Days 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
- **Set up the `CLOSER` keyword auto-DM first** so it instantly sends your closing system — that's the whole funnel.
