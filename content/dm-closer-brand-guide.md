# DM Closer — Brand Guide

*The Digital Closer.* A single source of truth for how the brand sounds, looks, and
sells — so every post, DM, email, and page feels like the same person.

---

## 1. Brand at a glance

- **Name:** DM Closer — *The Digital Closer*
- **One-liner:** I help course & digital-product creators turn conversations into customers.
- **Origin:** Learned to close at the hardest door in sales — 1,000+ cold, face-to-face closes — then translated the same moves to the DMs.
- **The promise:** You'll stop chasing leads and start calmly closing the ones you already have.

## 2. Positioning statement

> **For** course & digital-product creators who can build and launch but freeze when it's time to sell, **DM Closer** is the closing system that turns DM conversations into customers — **because** most creators nail the marketing and fumble the close.

## 3. Who it's for

Course creators, template/digital-product sellers, coaches and service providers who
get attention and launch — but **lose the sale in the DMs**. They don't need more
leads; they need to convert the ones they have.

## 4. The wedge (what makes us different)

Everyone teaches build / launch / traffic — it's crowded. **Closing is wide open.**
Our unfair advantage is a real closing system forged at the door (agree & redirect,
read the room, push-pull, assume the close) and translated to digital. **Lead with the
close. It's the part nobody else teaches.**

## 5. Brand voice & tone

Calm. Low. Direct. In control. We **serve, we don't chase.** Human to human — never a
script reciting itself. We shoot straight from the hip.

**Do:**
- Speak plainly and calmly. A period where others use five exclamation points.
- Be honest, even when it's the uncomfortable truth.
- Teach one sharp move, not a wall of hype.

**Don't:**
- Be pushy, fake, or salesy.
- Make or coach **income / earnings claims** — *ever*. Teach the move, show the process, never the money.
- Borrow someone else's framework — frame it as *how I close*.

**Sounds like:** "Not sure it's even a fit yet — can I ask what you're working on?"
**Not like:** "🔥 DM me NOW to 10X your revenue this week!!! 🔥"

## 6. Words we use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| close, conversation, decide, serve, calm, the move | hustle, grind, crush it, hack-the-algorithm hype |
| customers, buyers, students, fit | "make $X", income figures, earnings promises |
| straight, honest, human | pushy, salesy, manipulative tactics |

## 7. Messaging pillars (rotate weekly)

| Pillar | Covers |
|--------|--------|
| **Offer** | what digital product to sell, package, validate |
| **Launch** | launch runways, urgency without sleaze |
| **Traffic / Hooks** | reels, hooks, reach |
| **The Close** ⭐ | DMs, objections, conversion — *the signature* |
| **Story / Authority** | the door→digital origin, behind the scenes |

Front-load Offer/Launch/Traffic for reach; **The Close** is what makes us different.

## 10. The funnel (the engine)

Every piece of content ends the same way: a **curiosity/value hook** for reach →
caption value → **"Comment `CLOSER`"** → an auto-DM (ManyChat / Instagram keyword reply)
sends the DM closing system. The comment feeds the algorithm; the DM starts the sale.
**The hook wins attention; the offer lives in the caption. Keep them separate.**

## 11. Visual identity

Dark, warm, understated — gold on near-black. The look *is* the voice: calm, in control,
a little luxe. No loud gradients, no clutter.

**Colors**
@swatch #14110c Ink — primary background
@swatch #C9A227 Gold — primary accent (CTAs, highlights)
@swatch #d8b552 Gold Light — hover / secondary accent
@swatch #f4efe4 Cream — body text
@swatch #1d1913 Panel — cards / surfaces
@swatch #a89c84 Muted — labels, captions

**Type**
- **Headlines:** Cambria / Georgia — *serif*, calm and editorial.
- **Labels & UI:** Helvetica Neue / Arial — uppercase, wide letter-spacing.
- **Body:** Georgia serif.

**Feel:** gold accents used sparingly; lots of dark space; serif headlines; one clear action per screen.

## 12. How to use this guide (and why it matters)

- **Before you post / write / hire:** check it sounds like Section 5 and uses Section 6 words.
- **Onboarding help** (VA, editor, copywriter): hand them this — they'll match your voice without you re-explaining it every time.
- **Every caption** ends with the Section 10 funnel.
- **Anything visual** uses Section 11 colors + type.

**Why a brand guide pays off:**
1. **Consistency** — one recognizable voice across reels, DMs, emails, and your Skool.
2. **Speed** — no blank-page guessing; the hooks, pillars, and CTA are decided.
3. **Delegation** — anyone you bring on sounds like *you* on day one.
4. **Trust → sales** — a consistent brand feels credible, and credibility is what closes.
5. **Focus** — the wedge (closing) keeps you distinct in a sea of generic marketing advice.
