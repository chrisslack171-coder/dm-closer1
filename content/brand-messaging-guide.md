# DM Closer — Brand Messaging Guide

Positioning for an audience of **course & digital-product creators**, with
**closing as the wedge.** Everyone teaches build / launch / traffic — you own the
part nobody teaches: the **close**.

---

## The positioning (one line)

> **I help course & digital-product creators turn conversations into customers — because most creators can build and launch, but freeze when it's time to close.**

Broad enough to pull in the whole creator economy; sharp enough that *closing* is
clearly your lane.

## Who it's for

Course creators, template/digital-product sellers, and the coaches & marketers
around them — people who can get attention and launch, but **lose the sale in the
DMs.**

## The wedge (your unfair advantage)

Build, launch, traffic, hooks = crowded. **Closing / DM conversion = wide open.**
You came from the hardest room in sales (1,000+ door closes) and translated it to
the DMs. That origin is the proof — lead with it, never bury it.

## Voice & rules

- Calm, low, direct, in control. You **serve, you don't chase.** Human to human.
- Never pushy, fake, or salesy. **No income or earnings claims, ever** — teach the *move*, not the money.
- When you explain why something works, frame it as *how you close* — not someone else's system.

## Content pillars (rotate weekly)

| Pillar | Covers | Pulls in |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Offer** | what digital product to sell, package, validate | product creators |
| **Launch** | launch sequences, urgency-without-sleaze, launch content | marketers |
| **Traffic / Hooks** | reels, hooks, reach | broad audience |
| **The Close** ⭐ | DMs, objections, conversion — *your signature* | your buyers |
| **Story / Authority** | door→digital origin, behind the scenes | trust |

Front-load Traffic + Offer + Launch for reach; the **Close** pillar is what makes
you different and drives the sale.

## The funnel (every post ends here)

Broad / curiosity hook → caption value → **"Comment `CLOSER`"** → auto-DM (ManyChat
or IG keyword reply) instantly sends your DM closing system. The comment feeds the
algorithm; the auto-DM starts the close. **That comment→DM hand-off is your product
in action.**

## How the product fits

The **DM Closer app** is the wedge made tangible — the closing system you hand
someone the moment they comment `CLOSER`. The brand widens to "build/launch/sell
digital products"; the product stays laser-focused on the close. Keep the app's
own copy about closing — don't dilute it.
