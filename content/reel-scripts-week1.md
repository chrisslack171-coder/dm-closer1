# DM Closer — Week 1 Reel Scripts

Built from the @misscarolineflett research. Her winning formula: a **broad-appeal
or curiosity hook** (the spoken hook is never the offer) to win reach, then the
**niche offer lives only in the caption** via a **comment-a-keyword → DM** funnel.
We apply the same machine to your world — closing in the DMs.

**Your funnel keyword: `CLOSER`.** Set an auto-DM (ManyChat / Instagram's native
keyword reply) so that when someone comments `CLOSER`, they're sent your DM
closing system. That comment→DM step IS your product.

**Voice:** calm, low, direct, in control. You serve, you don't chase. Human to
human. **No income or earnings claims — ever.** Teach the *move*, not the money.

**Cadence:** 1 reel/day below (Caroline posts 2/day — double up by reusing a
broad hack reel + a teaching reel on the same day once you're rolling).

---

## Day 1 — Origin / authority

- **Hook (say it, first line):** "I learned to close at the hardest door in sales — a stranger who wanted me gone. The same move closes cold DMs."
- **On-screen text:** The hardest close in sales 👇
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "When someone's reaching for the door — or leaving you on read — you never fight the 'not interested.' You agree, then redirect. *'Totally — that's actually why I reached out.'* Then one real reason you're there. No pitch. Just curiosity. The door taught me that, and it works the exact same in the DMs."
- **Caption:** The door taught me to close — calm, no chasing. Now I do it in the DMs. Comment **CLOSER** and I'll send you my DM closing system. 🤝 Save this · Follow for more.
- **Why it works:** origin + proof hook (like her "journey" reels) builds authority; keyword funnels the reach.

## Day 2 — Broad-reach phone hack (reach play)

- **Hook:** "Phone trick for anyone who sells in the DMs — I wish I knew this sooner."
- **On-screen text:** Reply 3× faster 👇
- **Format:** screen-recording demo
- **Script:** Show iPhone **Text Replacement** (Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement). Save your best DM openers and objection responses as shortcuts — type `;open` and your full opener appears. "Never retype your best lines again."
- **Caption:** Stop retyping the same DMs all day. Speed = more conversations = more closes. Comment **CLOSER** for the actual words I use to close in the DMs. Save this 🔖
- **Why it works:** a broadly useful hack = max reach (her top reels were phone hacks); still relevant to sellers; funnels to your niche.

## Day 3 — The objection line (one sharp mechanic)

- **Hook:** "'Let me think about it' is never about thinking. Here's the one line that flips it."
- **On-screen text:** The think-about-it close
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "When they say 'let me think about it,' don't push — lower your tone. *'Totally fair. Usually when someone says that, there's one specific thing they're unsure about. What is it for you?'* Now you're handling the real objection, not the smokescreen."
- **Caption:** The objection they say is rarely the real one. Comment **CLOSER** for my full objection playbook. Save this for your next DM.
- **Why it works:** contrarian + curiosity hook; one clean, usable mechanic beats a list of fluff.

## Day 4 — Listicle: the 3 people in your DMs

- **Hook:** "There are only 3 types of people in your DMs — and you close each one differently."
- **On-screen text:** 1 · 2 · 3 👇
- **Format:** listicle talking head (count on fingers / text overlays)
- **Script:** "One — the window shopper. Watches everything, never replies. Don't pitch; open a real conversation. Two — the tire-kicker. Fishes for free advice. Don't just answer — ask about their situation first. Three — the ready buyer. Already leaning in. Stop selling and assume the close: offer two times and let them pick."
- **Caption:** Most people close all three the same way — and lose two of them. Comment **CLOSER** for the script for each. 🔖 Save this.
- **Why it works:** numbered/listicle = save-worthy (her "4 strategies / 6 hacks" format).

## Day 5 — Stop chasing (push-pull)

- **Hook:** "Stop chasing leads in the DMs. Watch what happens when you pull back."
- **On-screen text:** Push → pull
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "Give value, then take a little away. *'Honestly, not sure this is even a fit for you yet — can I ask what you're working on?'* That bit of uncertainty makes them start selling YOU on why it's a fit. Chasing kills the close. Calm wins it."
- **Caption:** Desperation repels. Calm closes. Comment **CLOSER** for the push-pull DMs I use. Follow for more.
- **Why it works:** pattern-interrupt hook; teaches your signature push-pull mechanic.

## Day 6 — Assume the close / option close

- **Hook:** "Never ask 'do you want to buy.' Ask this instead."
- **On-screen text:** Two yeses, never a yes/no
- **Format:** talking head
- **Script:** "Once they've leaned in, don't ask *if*. Assume it. *'I've got Tuesday or Thursday to get you started — which works better?'* Two yeses, never a yes-or-no. You're not selling anymore. You're just scheduling."
- **Caption:** The close isn't a question — it's an assumption. Comment **CLOSER** for the exact close-out messages. Save this 🔖
- **Why it works:** bold contrarian command hook; instantly usable line.

## Day 7 — The re-engage DM (story + broad relatability)

- **Hook:** "Someone left me on read for 6 days. Here's the message that woke them back up."
- **On-screen text:** The re-engage DM
- **Format:** talking head (or screen-rec of a mock thread)
- **Script:** "No guilt. No 'just following up.' *'Hey [name] — no pressure at all, I'll assume the timing's just off. If that changes, I'm right here. Genuinely rooting for you either way.'* You give them the easy out — and that's exactly why they come back."
- **Caption:** 'Just following up' is where deals die. Comment **CLOSER** for my re-engage scripts. Save this for the next ghost. 👻
- **Why it works:** universally relatable pain (being ghosted) + curiosity hook = shares; honest, serve-first move on-brand.

## Filming notes (steal Caroline's mechanics)

- **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds**, said out loud AND as on-screen text. The hook is broad/curious; the offer stays in the caption.
- **Keep it ~20–40 seconds.** One idea per reel.
- **Every caption ends with `Comment CLOSER`** + "Save this" + "Follow." The comment spikes the algorithm; the auto-DM starts the close.
- **Mix reach + authority:** alternate a broad hack/story reel (Days 2, 7) with a teaching reel (Days 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
- **Set up the keyword auto-DM first** (ManyChat or IG keyword reply) so `CLOSER` instantly sends your system — that's the whole funnel.
