# DM Closer — Ready-to-Post Reels (your voice)

Built on @misscarolineflett's proven formats, written in the Digital Closer voice:
calm, low, direct, from the hip. You serve, you don't chase. No scripts-that-sound-
like-scripts. **No income or earnings claims.** Audience: course & digital-product
creators. Funnel: every caption ends with **comment `CLOSER`** → auto-DM sends your
system.

> **On-screen text** = exactly what to put on the screen (one line per beat, ~1.5–2s each).
> **Sound** = pick a *small* trending audio (under ~10K uses) so the algorithm pushes it.
> Most of these need **zero filming** — text over audio, like her biggest reel.
